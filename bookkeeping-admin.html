Financial reporting status

Production opening journals are posted and included in the official ledger. The current opening baseline is two posted entries: the shareholder loan received on 20 November 2026 and the legal search fee paid on 24 November 2026. The Trial Balance, Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet and Bank Cash Movement reports use posted journal entries only.

Expense and Shareholder Loan Register rows are separate supporting records linked to their posted journal entries. A missing register row does not mean the journal is a draft, and must never be corrected by posting the same transaction again. Register write access is restricted to the Super Administrator.
